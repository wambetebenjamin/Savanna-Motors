Manufacturer service intervals are written for smooth tarmac, clean fuel and moderate dust.
Nairobi traffic, Mombasa Road construction and upcountry murram are a different operating
environment, and your service book should reflect that.

## Oil: halve the interval in town

A 10,000 km interval assumes the engine spends most of its life at steady cruising speed. A
car that crawls from Rongai to the CBD every morning accumulates far more engine hours per
kilometre than the book assumes.

- Stop-start town driving: change oil every 5,000 km
- Mixed town and highway: 7,500 km
- Mostly highway: the full manufacturer interval is fine

Hybrids are not exempt. The engine still runs, it just runs in short bursts, which is harder
on oil than continuous running.

## Air filters are a dust problem, not a time problem

During the dry season we routinely pull filters that are completely packed at 8,000 km. Check
the filter at every oil change and replace it whenever you cannot see light through it. A
restricted filter costs you fuel long before it triggers a warning light.

## Suspension: the real Kenyan wear item

Speed bumps, potholes and loaded boots destroy bushes, links and shock absorbers faster than
anything else on the car. Have the following inspected every 10,000 km:

- Shock absorbers for oil weep
- Lower arm and anti-roll bar bushes
- Steering rack ends and ball joints
- Wheel alignment, which drifts after every serious pothole

## Brakes and fluid

Brake fluid is hygroscopic — it absorbs moisture from the air, and humid coastal and lakeside
air accelerates that. Test the fluid annually and change it every two years regardless of
mileage. On long descents such as the Nairobi–Naivasha escarpment, fluid with high moisture
content will fade exactly when you need it.

## Fuel system

Fuel quality varies. If the car hesitates under load or the idle is rough, get a diagnostic
scan before replacing parts. Nine times out of ten the fix is a clean, a filter and a good
tank of fuel rather than new injectors.

| Interval | Work |

| Every 10,000 km | Air and cabin filters, suspension check, alignment |
